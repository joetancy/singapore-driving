About this demo map

This release uses illustrative road and building GeoJSON around Marina Bay. It is geographically approximate, not sourced from OSM, and does not cover all of Singapore. OSM downloads were unavailable during the build. The engine is ready for a real Singapore OSM export. Buildings, heights, road widths and landmarks are simplified.
